Every neuron, expression pattern, region or template you add to the [3D Viewer](/docs/website-features/3dviewer/) appears as a row in the Layers tab, with its name, type and a [thumbnail](/docs/website-features/thumbnails/). Click a name to select it and open its [Term Info](/docs/website-features/terminfo/); click the type to open the Term Info for that class.

Each row has a controls menu with:

- **Show Info** – open the item's Term Info.
- **Select** / **Unselect** – highlight the item in the viewers.
- **Show** / **Hide** – toggle its visibility without removing it.
- **Zoom To** – centre the 3D camera on it.
- **Show Volume** and **Show Skeleton** – for items with both a mesh and a skeleton, choose which representation to draw.
- **Color** – recolour the item.
- **Delete** – remove it from the viewers.

The list updates as you add images from queries, the Term Info thumbnails or the Template ROI Browser.
