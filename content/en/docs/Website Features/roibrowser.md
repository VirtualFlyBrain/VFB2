The Template ROI Browser shows the painted domains (regions of interest) of the template currently loaded in the [3D Viewer](/docs/website-features/3dviewer/), arranged as a tree that follows the anatomy ontology's part-of hierarchy: expand a neuropil to see its sub-regions. Hover a row to see a short description of that region.

<i class="fa fa-eye"></i> / <i class="fa fa-eye-slash"></i>   Load a region into the 3D and [Slice](/docs/website-features/sliceviewer/) viewers, or show/hide one that is already loaded

<i class="fa fa-tint"></i>   Change the colour of a loaded region

Click a region's name to open its [Term Info](/docs/website-features/terminfo/). Switching template (for example from the adult brain to the VNC or the larval CNS) reloads the tree for the new template.
