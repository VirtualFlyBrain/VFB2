Canonical templates allow image data to be spatially aligned, and are often *painted* to
make a reference atlas of anatomical regions. Many central nervous system (CNS) templates
exist for *Drosophila*; below is a summary of those used on VFB, grouped by the part of
the nervous system and life stage they cover. Each template and its painted domains can be
viewed and downloaded from their [Term Info](/docs/website-features/terminfo/#data) pages.

## Adult brain templates

### JRC2018 unisex adult brain
<img src="https://virtualflybrain.org/data/VFB/i/0010/1567/VFB_00101567/thumbnail.png" alt="JRC2018 unisex adult brain template thumbnail" style="max-width:35%">

The **current standard** adult brain template on VFB. An unbiased, averaged template built from many individual brains by [Bogovic et al., 2020](https://flybase.org/reports/FBrf0247641); most adult brain image data on VFB is aligned to it. Original files: [JRC 2018 Brain templates](https://www.janelia.org/open-science/jrc-2018-brain-templates). [View on VFB](https://virtualflybrain.org/reports/VFB_00101567).

## Adult ventral nerve cord templates

### JRC2018 unisex VNC
<img src="https://virtualflybrain.org/data/VFB/i/0020/0000/VFB_00200000/thumbnail.png" alt="JRC2018 unisex VNC template thumbnail" style="max-width:35%">

The **current standard** adult ventral nerve cord (VNC) template — the VNC counterpart to the JRC2018 unisex brain, built by combining male and female confocal images ([Bogovic et al., 2020](https://flybase.org/reports/FBrf0247641)). Adult VNC connectome data (e.g. MANC) is aligned to it; its neuropil regions are available from the template's [Term Info](/docs/website-features/terminfo/#data) page. [View on VFB](https://virtualflybrain.org/reports/VFB_00200000).

<img src="https://virtualflybrain.org/data/VFB/i/0010/0000/VFB_00100000/thumbnail.png" alt="Adult VNS — Court2018 template thumbnail" style="max-width:35%">

An adult VNC atlas ([Court et al., 2020](https://flybase.org/reports/FBrf0246815)): annotated confocal images registered to a common coordinate space, providing a standard adult VNC neuropil and tract nomenclature. [View on VFB](https://virtualflybrain.org/reports/VFB_00100000).
